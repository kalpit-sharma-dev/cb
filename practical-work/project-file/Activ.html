Weekly Activities

Recycle Club — Kids Club

The Recycle Club of N R Public School meets every week. Students of Class 10 can join. These are our activities.

Day Activity
Monday Collect waste paper from each classroom.
Tuesday Separate wet waste and dry waste near the garden.
Wednesday Make paper bags from old sheets.
Thursday Give a short talk in the morning assembly.
Friday Pick up litter from the playground and water the plants.
Saturday Make a poster on Reduce, Reuse and Recycle.

Come with a smile and a pair of gloves. A clean school is our duty.
