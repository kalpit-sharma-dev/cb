Recycle City

A clean city begins with us

Recycle City is a dream city where people do not throw waste on the road. The parks are green, the drains are clear and children know the 3 Rs.

In Recycle City

  • Every house has two dustbins, one for wet waste and one for dry waste.
  • Shops give cloth bags. They do not give plastic bags.
  • Schools teach students to reuse old books and bottles.
  • Factories recycle paper, plastic and metal.
  • People plant a tree on their birthday.

Our school, N R Public School, Junction Road, Khurja, wants our own city to become clean like Recycle City. We can start from our desk, our classroom and our street.
