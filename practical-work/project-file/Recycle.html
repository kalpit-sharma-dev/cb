Meaning of Reduce, Recycle and Reuse

Reduce, Reuse and Recycle are called the 3 Rs. They are three easy ways to make less waste and protect nature.

Reduce

Reduce means to use less, so that we create less waste. We should buy only what we need.

  • Carry a cloth bag when you go to the market.
  • Write on both sides of the paper.
  • Switch off fans and lights when you leave the room.

Reuse

Reuse means to use a thing again instead of throwing it away.

  • Use an old jar to keep pencils.
  • Give old story books to a younger student.
  • Cover your notebooks with old calendars.

Recycle

Recycle means sending old material to a place where it is made into something new. Paper, plastic, glass and metal can be recycled.

  • Old newspapers can become new paper.
  • Glass bottles can be melted and shaped again.
  • Steel tins can be used to make new metal things.

If every student follows the 3 Rs at home and at school, our city Khurja will be cleaner.
