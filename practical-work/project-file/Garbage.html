What is Garbage?

Garbage is the waste we throw away when we do not need it any more. Paper, plastic bottles, fruit peels and broken things are all garbage if we put them in the dustbin.

Two kinds of garbage

  1. Biodegradable waste: This waste rots and mixes with the soil. Examples are vegetable peels, leaves and leftover food.
  2. Non-biodegradable waste: This waste does not rot easily. Examples are plastic, glass and metal.

If we leave garbage in the open, it smells bad and flies sit on it. It can make people ill. We should throw waste only in a dustbin and keep our classroom and street clean.

At N R Public School we keep two bins in each class. One bin is for wet waste and the other is for dry waste.
