Subjects I Study

N R Public School, Junction Road, Khurja — Class 10

Science
Science tells us how things in nature work. In Class 10 we study light, electricity, chemical reactions and life processes. My favourite subject is Science.
Mathematics
Mathematics is the subject of numbers. We learn algebra, geometry and trigonometry. It also helps me while making tables in HTML.
Computer Applications
In this subject we learn HTML. We make web pages using headings, lists, tables, images, links and forms.
English
English helps us read stories and write answers in clear sentences. It is also the language we use for most computer words.
Social Science
Social Science has History, Geography, Civics and Economics. It helps us know our country and the world around us.
