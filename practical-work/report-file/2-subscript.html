Subscript and Superscript Example

Chemical equation

Carbon dioxide reacts with water to form carbonic acid.

CO2 + H2O → H2CO3

Mathematical expression

This is the formula for the square of a sum of two numbers.

(a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2

The numbers written a little below the line use the sub tag. The numbers written a little above the line use the sup tag.
